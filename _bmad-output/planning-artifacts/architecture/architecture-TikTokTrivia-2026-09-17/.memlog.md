The draft prescribed style_reference_images + style_codes + seed; that is wrong twice. style_reference_images does NOT exist on fal (it is image_urls; the other name belongs to Ideogram's direct API, which was not chosen), and style_codes' own description says 'Cannot be used in conjunction with style_reference_images or style' -- mutually exclusive.
